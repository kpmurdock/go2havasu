LAKE HAVASU CITY · ARIZONA

Find your next Havasu adventure.

Iconic bridge views, sunny water, desert trails, and Colorado River escapes. Confirm fees, hours, and access on the official pages before you go.

Explore destinations

The London Bridge

The relocated London Bridge is the city’s landmark: a walk across the span, waterfront shops, and boats passing through the Bridgewater Channel.

Walk the bridge

Cross the historic bridge, then wander the English Village side for photos, dining, and channel views.

Visitor information

Bridgewater Channel

Watch boats, stroll the waterfront, and use the channel as home base between the bridge and the lake.

Explore Havasu

Boating & water fun

Lake Havasu is the reason most people visit: boating, fishing, swimming, and long desert shorelines. Check wind, heat, and launch rules the day you go.

On the lake

Powerboats, personal watercraft, fishing, and swimming share the water. Give swimmers space and follow posted no-wake zones.

Lake visitor guide

Lake Havasu State Park

Shoreline recreation, beaches, camping, and lake views close to town.

Official park information

Hiking & outdoors

Desert trails heat up fast. Start early, carry more water than you think you need, and turn back if the route is exposed.

Buckskin Mountain State Park

Colorado River scenery, hiking, and camping along the Parker Strip, with views back toward the lake country.

Official park information

Parker Dam & the Colorado River

A scenic drive to the dam area. Follow posted restrictions and access rules.

Reclamation information

Havasu National Wildlife Refuge

Protected Colorado River habitat and wildlife viewing where public access is allowed.

Official refuge information

State parks & areas

Cattail Cove State Park

A quieter lake getaway south of the city for camping, swimming, paddling, and desert shoreline.

Official park information

Sunset & photo spots

Golden hour on the bridge, the channel, and west-facing desert ridges. Photos on this site are still being replaced with licensed Lake Havasu images.

Plan your visit

Dining & nightlife

Go2Havasu is not listing individual restaurants until details are confirmed. Use the London Bridge waterfront as the practical starting point for meals, and check hours before you go.

Places to stay

Lodging partners are not booked on this site. Camping is available at Lake Havasu State Park and Cattail Cove when sites are open. Hotels and vacation rentals are concentrated around the bridge and the lake.

Lake Havasu State Park camping

Visitor tip: Bring drinking water and sun protection. Check weather, heat, boating conditions, and park notices before outdoor activities. This site does not sell tickets or take payment.
