# Go2Havasu — 12-month business development plan

## Positioning
A useful independent guide to Lake Havasu City, London Bridge, Colorado River parks and regional outdoor activities, paired with branded merchandise and local business referrals.

## Revenue streams (launch in stages)
1. Branded caps, shirts and mugs through a print-on-demand storefront with hosted checkout. Validate product margin after production, shipping, payment fees, returns and advertising.
2. Sponsored destination listings and clearly labeled paid features; keep editorial recommendations separate.
3. Affiliate referrals to eligible lodging, tours and activities, with prominent FTC-compliant disclosures.
4. Downloadable itineraries, maps and seasonal visitor guides.
5. Later: display ads once traffic is substantial enough to justify their impact on site experience.

## Content pillars
- London Bridge and Bridgewater Channel: walking tours, history, restaurants and photos.
- Water: boating, beaches, kayaking, lake safety and seasonal guidance.
- Parks: Lake Havasu State Park, Cattail Cove and Buckskin Mountain.
- Regional drives: Parker Dam, Colorado River, wildlife and desert viewpoints.
- Practical visitor information: accessibility, weather, heat precautions, parking and itineraries.

## Social funnel
Publish three to five short original vertical videos weekly across Instagram Reels and Facebook, with clear links to relevant Go2Havasu guide pages. Create one longer destination guide monthly for YouTube. Use a consistent branded end card and captions. Obtain location/filming permissions and music licenses where needed. Do not represent AI-generated visuals as documentary footage.

## Site priorities
1. Confirm GitHub-to-Vercel deployment and production DNS/HTTPS; avoid simultaneous GitHub Pages custom-domain ownership.
2. Add destination landing pages, original images with alt text, descriptive URLs, structured metadata, sitemap and analytics.
3. Integrate a hosted, PCI-compliant checkout; never collect payment card numbers directly on the static site.
4. Connect a consent-based email provider with double opt-in and privacy/terms pages.
5. Add real social profile URLs after profiles are confirmed.
6. Publish a sponsorship media kit and contact form with spam protection.

## Milestones
- Months 1–2: finish site foundation, eight attraction pages, analytics, newsletter and first 12 reels.
- Months 3–4: launch merchandise checkout, produce two long-form videos, test organic social referrals.
- Months 5–6: approach local businesses for sponsorship and referral agreements; optimize high-traffic pages.
- Months 7–9: add seasonal itineraries and local event coverage; test small ad budgets against actual contribution margin.
- Months 10–12: scale the content and partnerships that convert; discontinue unprofitable campaigns.

## KPIs
Organic sessions, social referral clicks, email opt-in rate, guide-to-partner clicks, merchandise conversion rate, average order value, gross margin, sponsor revenue and net profit. Set targets after baseline measurement; do not assume traffic or revenue.

## Editorial and compliance
Verify park access, fees, hours and business claims before publishing. Use original or properly licensed photography and video; label illustrative AI art. Disclose affiliate relationships and sponsored placements. Review Arizona business registration, sales-tax obligations and privacy compliance with qualified professionals.
